01 · The stack04

// start here

Your brand doc is your agents' instruction manual.

✓

Positioning & messaging

Who you serve, what you do, and why you over the alternative. One paragraph.

✓

Voice & tone

Three words that describe how you sound, with one "we say this, not that" example each.

✓

Words you never use

The jargon, clichés and claims that don't sound like you.

✓

Color, type & theme

Exact colors and fonts, so every graphic and page matches.

✓

Proof you can point to

Results, credentials and examples agents are allowed to use. Nothing invented.

The testIf a new hire couldn't write a good post from your brand doc, an agent can't either.

03 · Agent seats07

// write it down once

Teach the agent your workflow in plain English.

A skill is one file the agent reads before it does a job. Here's part of the real one I use to publish my site.

.claude/skills/update-site/SKILL.md
## Step 5: Pull request
Say: "Opening a pull request - this asks for the
change to be added to the live site. Vinny will
post a review card for it in Slack."

## Step 6: Review and merge
Stop here and let Simonne review.
Only merge it yourself if she asks.
Side effectIf you can't write the steps down, an agent can't follow them. Writing them down usually exposes the step you've been skipping.

02 · The review loop09

// what the demos skip

Where agents still fall short, and what I check.

01

They're confidently wrong.

An agent-written note in my own setup named the wrong website as the live one. Now my publishing skill names the right domain, and says which one isn't it.

02

They'll invent proof.

My Kickoff Brief tells agents in writing: don't invent reviews, numbers, credentials or results. Leave a marked placeholder instead.

03

They don't notice contradictions.

A playful voice with formal fonts will get built as-is. I added a step that flags answers that pull against each other, to settle with a person first.

04

They default to generic.

The first draft of an intro post for this series was the same "AI drafts, a human approves" diagram everyone posts. It got cut.

What stays with mePositioning, pricing, what gets published and when. Agents speed up the work. They don't make the calls.

04 · Rollout10

// one piece a day

Your 5-day rollout plan.

Day 1brand

Write your brand doc

Use the checklist on page 4. An hour of honest notes beats a perfect PDF.

Day 2measure

Turn on measurement

Analytics, a tag manager and a sitemap, and update your privacy policy to match.

Day 3capture

Connect intake to your CRM

Every form sends to one place. Test it with a real submission.

Day 4review

Set up your review loop

Decide where drafts wait and who says yes. Nothing goes live without it.

Day 5teach

Write your first skill

Pick the task you repeat most and write the steps the way you'd explain them to a new hire.
